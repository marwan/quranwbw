### Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 11, 2026_

QuranWBW.com is a passion project built to help people read, listen to, and learn the Quran. We collect as little as possible. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no ads, and we never sell your data. We also do not ask for or accept donations or payments of any kind, so we never handle any payment or financial information.

This page explains what is stored, where, and why.

---

### Stored On Your Device

Most of what you do on QuranWBW stays in your own browser and never reaches our servers. This includes your display settings, font sizes, theme, bookmarks, favourites, reading history, last-read position, and any downloaded audio or fonts for offline use.

Clearing your browser data removes all of it. Cloudflare Turnstile may also keep small security-check data in your browser, as described under Cookies & Local Storage below.

---

### Cloud Backup & Restore

Cloud backup is optional. Nothing is sent to our servers unless you choose to generate or enter a backup key on the [Backup & Restore](/backup) page.

#### What we store

- Your backup key: a randomly generated key (for example `pal10-hop30-sky21-key28`). It is the only thing that identifies your backup. We do not ask for a name or email.
- A copy of your settings: the settings saved on your device at the time you press Backup. These may include preferences such as bookmarks, favourites and reading position, which can reveal personal religious practice, so we treat them with care.
- Timestamps: when the key was created and when your settings were last backed up, using your local timezone.
- Technical data: if you use cloud backup and restore, our server logs technical details such as your IP address, browser details, and request times to deliver requests, fix errors, and prevent abuse. These logs are automatically deleted after 60 days and are not used for anything else. We do not keep these server logs when you use the rest of the website without cloud backup.

#### Why we store it

- To let you restore your settings on another device or after clearing your browser.
- To keep the service safe from spam and abuse.

We do not use your backup for anything else. We do not read it, analyse it, share it, or sell it.

#### How long we keep it

- Backup keys that are not backed up, restored or used for 30 days are permanently deleted from the cloud, along with the settings saved under them.
- Pressing the "Delete" button on the Backup & Restore page only removes the key from your device. Your cloud backup stays until it expires or you ask us to delete it.

#### Important things to know

- Your key is your password. Anyone who has your key can restore or overwrite your backup. Keep it private and do not post it publicly.
- We cannot recover a lost key. We have no email or account to verify who you are, so a lost key cannot be reset.
- Restoring replaces settings. Restoring a backup replaces the settings on that device. Some device-specific settings, such as font sizes and offline mode, are never overwritten.
- Local file backups are separate. The local export on the same page creates a file on your device and does not contact our servers. The file is only lightly encoded, not encrypted, so treat it as private.
- Cloud backup is in beta. It is provided as is, and we cannot guarantee it will always be available or that a backup will never be lost. We recommend also keeping a local file backup.

#### Requesting deletion

To delete your cloud backup before the 30 days are up, email us at [quranwbw@gmail.com](mailto:quranwbw@gmail.com) with your backup key. The key is the only way we can confirm the backup is yours.

Once we receive it, we will delete the backup and the key. Please delete the email afterwards so the key does not stay in your inbox. If you do not have your key, we will not be able to locate or delete the backup, and it will expire on its own after 30 days of inactivity.

### Security

Data sent to our server is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. We take reasonable steps to protect stored backups, but no system is completely secure, and backups are not end-to-end encrypted.

To protect certain pages and features from bots and abuse, we use [Cloudflare Turnstile](https://www.cloudflare.com/products/turnstile/). It runs a quick check in the background when you use those features. Cloudflare may process technical signals such as your IP address and browser information to do this. Their handling of that data is described in the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

---

### Analytics (Rybbit)

We use [Rybbit](https://rybbit.io/), a privacy-focused analytics tool, to understand how QuranWBW is used.

We would honestly prefer not to track anything. But this is a small, independent project with no team or user research budget, and analytics are the only way we can see what is working and what is not. For example, they help us answer questions like:

- Which features do people actually use, and which ones go unnoticed?
- Do people run into errors, such as a failed backup?
- Which devices and screen sizes should we design for?
- Is a new feature worth the time we put into it?

This lets us spend our limited time improving what helps you most, instead of guessing.

#### What is and is not collected

- Collected: page views, general device and browser type, country and region, and named events such as "Cloud Backup Success" or "Export Settings". Your IP address is not stored by Rybbit.
- Not collected: the content of your settings, your backup key, your bookmarks, or your reading history. Events only record that an action happened, not what was in it.
- Not done: we do not use analytics for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or share the data with advertisers.

### Third-Party Content & Services

Most of the content on QuranWBW, including Quran text, translations, word by word data, fonts, and word by word audio, is served from our own content delivery network hosted on [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/). Like any hosting provider, Cloudflare processes technical details such as your IP address to deliver these files. See their [Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

A few features rely on external providers (see the [About](/about) page for the full list):

- Ayah by ayah audio: your browser connects directly to the audio provider to stream the recitation.
- Tafsir: your browser may connect directly to the tafsir provider to load the content.
- Search: search queries are sent to the search provider to return results.

These providers can see technical details such as your IP address. We do not control how they handle that data, so please see their own privacy policies.

### Cookies & Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage and IndexedDB to remember your settings and keep features working offline. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Cloudflare Turnstile (see Security above) may also store small pieces of data in your browser's local storage to complete its security check. This is set by Cloudflare, not by us, and is used only for bot and abuse protection.

### Children

QuranWBW is open to everyone. We do not knowingly collect personal information such as names or emails from anyone, including children.

### Your Choices

- Use QuranWBW without ever creating a backup key. Nothing is sent to our backup server.
- Use local file backup instead of cloud backup.
- Delete your key from your device at any time.
- Ask us to delete your cloud backup (see above).
- Block analytics with your browser's tracking protection or an extension. The website will keep working.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because backups are identified only by key, we can act on a request only if you send us your backup key.
