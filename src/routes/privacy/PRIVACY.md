### Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 23, 2026_

QuranWBW.com is a passion project built to help people read, listen to, and learn the Quran. We collect as little as possible. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no ads, and we never sell your data. We also do not ask for or accept donations or payments of any kind, so we never handle any payment or financial information.

This page explains what is stored, where, and why.

---

### Stored On Your Device

Most of what you do on QuranWBW.com stays in your own browser and never reaches our servers. This includes your display settings, font sizes, theme, bookmarks, favourites, notes, reading history, last-read position, quiz progress, and any downloaded audio or fonts for offline use.

Clearing your browser data removes all of it.

---

### Analytics (Rybbit)

We use [Rybbit](https://rybbit.io/), a privacy-focused analytics tool, to understand how QuranWBW.com is used. Rybbit runs on our own server, so this data is not handed to a third-party analytics company.

We would honestly prefer not to track anything. But this is a small, independent project with no team or user research budget, and analytics are the only way we can see what is working and what is not. For example, they help us answer questions like:

- Which features do people actually use, and which ones go unnoticed?
- Which devices and screen sizes should we design for?
- Is a new feature worth the time we put into it?

This lets us spend our limited time improving what helps you most, instead of guessing.

#### What is and is not collected

- Collected: page views, general device and browser type, country and region, and named events such as "Add Chapter To Favorites". Your IP address is not stored by Rybbit.
- Not collected: the content of your settings, your bookmarks, your notes, or your reading history. Events only record that an action happened, not what was in it.
- Not done: we do not use analytics for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or share the data with advertisers.
- Rybbit does not use cookies to identify you.

#### Turning analytics off

You can turn this off at any time under "Share Anonymous Stats" in the website settings. When you turn it off, the analytics script is no longer loaded at all, so nothing is sent to Rybbit from your browser. The page reloads when you change this setting.

Your choice is remembered only in your own browser (see Cookies & Local Storage below). If you clear your browser data or use a different browser or device, you will need to turn it off again.

---

### Third-Party Content & Services

Most of the content on QuranWBW.com, including Quran text, translations, word by word data, fonts, and word by word audio, is served from our own content delivery network hosted on [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/). Like any hosting provider, Cloudflare processes technical details such as your IP address to deliver these files. See their [Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

A few features rely on external providers (see the [About](/about) page for the full list):

- Ayah by ayah audio: your browser connects directly to the audio provider to stream the recitation.
- Tafsir: your browser may connect directly to the tafsir provider to load the content.
- Search: search queries are sent to the search provider to return results.

These providers can see technical details such as your IP address. We do not control how they handle that data, so please see their own privacy policies.

---

### Cookies & Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage and IndexedDB to remember your settings and keep features working offline. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

If you turn off analytics, we store a small flag in your browser's local storage so your choice is remembered. It is never sent anywhere.

---

### Children

QuranWBW.com is open to everyone. We do not knowingly collect personal information such as names or emails from anyone, including children.

---

### Your Choices

- Use QuranWBW.com without any account. There is nothing to sign up for.
- Turn off anonymous usage stats from the website settings at any time.
- Block analytics with your browser's tracking protection or an extension. The website will keep working.
- Clear your browser data to remove everything stored on your device.

---

### Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

QuranWBW.com does not collect names, emails, or any identifier that links usage data to a specific person. This means we cannot tell which analytics data came from you, so we are unable to locate, correct, or delete it on request. Your settings, bookmarks, and reading history are stored only on your own device, and you can remove them at any time by clearing your browser data.

What you can do is stop future collection by turning off "Share Anonymous Stats" in the website settings. You are also welcome to get in touch with any questions.
